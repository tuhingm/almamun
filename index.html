Academic profile

Economist researching statistical methodology, Bayesian inference, computational statistics, and structural macroeconomic modeling. My work combines statistical estimation, dynamic systems, numerical methods, and scientific computing to investigate monetary and fiscal transmission, macroeconomic fluctuations, household behavior, and environmental outcomes.

Research approaches include DSGE and HANK frameworks, hierarchical modeling, Bayesian computation, parameter heterogeneity, likelihood estimation, MCMC, numerical optimization, and simulation.
