Academic profile

I am an economist with research interests in statistical methodology, Bayesian inference, computational statistics, and structural macroeconomic modeling. My research combines statistical estimation, dynamic systems, numerical methods, and scientific computing. I use these tools to study monetary and fiscal policy transmission, macroeconomic fluctuations, household behavior, and environmental outcomes.

I work with Dynamic Stochastic General Equilibrium (DSGE) models and Heterogeneous-Agent New Keynesian (HANK) frameworks. These models help me study policy dynamics, economic uncertainty, and heterogeneous responses to macroeconomic shocks.

I am particularly interested in hierarchical modeling. My interests also include Bayesian computation, estimation under structural heterogeneity, and quantitative methods for complex systems. My computational work includes likelihood-based estimation, Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods, numerical optimization, and simulation. I use Python, Julia, R, and MATLAB/Dynare.

Research profile

Research Interests

Six areas of expertise

01

Time-Series and State-Space Models

  • VAR, SVAR, VECM, ARDL, Smooth Transition Regression (STR), and threshold autoregression.
  • Dynamic simultaneous-equations models and state-space filtering using the Kalman filter.
  • Structural time-series analysis of nonlinear dynamics and regime-dependent relationships.
02

Dynamic Panel Models

  • Fixed Effects, Random Effects, and Dynamic Fixed Effects (DFE).
  • Panel VAR, Panel VECM, and dynamic panel Generalized Method of Moments (GMM).
  • Mean Group (MG), Pooled Mean Group (PMG), and Panel CCE-ARDL.
  • Panel ARDL with error-correction modeling (ECM).
03

Bayesian Factor Models

  • Dynamic Factor Models and Factor-Augmented Bayesian Panel Mean Group estimation.
  • Bayesian VECM with shrinkage priors.
  • Bayesian hierarchical modeling and MCMC posterior estimation.
  • Bayesian VAR and VECM estimation with structural breaks.
04

Causal Inference and Structural Breaks

  • Chow and Bai–Perron structural-break models.
  • Counterfactual path estimation, generalized synthetic control, and Difference-in-Differences (DiD).
  • Bivariate and panel Granger causality, and Johansen and panel cointegration tests.
  • Panel model estimation with structural breaks.
05

Structural Macroeconomic Models

  • Dynamic Stochastic General Equilibrium (DSGE) models, first-order perturbation, and steady-state calibration.
  • Continuous-time Heterogeneous-Agent New Keynesian (HANK) models using coupled Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman (HJB) and Kolmogorov forward equations.
  • Taylor-rule and fiscal reaction-function models.
  • Dynamic cohort analysis and dependency-ratio models.
06

Machine Learning and Numerical Optimization

  • Lasso and Ridge regression (L1/L2 regularization), cross-validated tuning, and high-dimensional parameter reduction.
  • Regularized machine learning and Random Forest for predictive modeling.
  • Newton’s method, Broyden–Fletcher–Goldfarb–Shanno (BFGS), Limited-Memory BFGS (L-BFGS), and Linear Programming (LP).
