Research portfolio · 2023–Present

Working Papers

A collection of ongoing research in environmental and energy economics, monetary institutions, macro-finance, structural macroeconomic modelling, and applied econometrics.

15 manuscriptsResearch period: 2023–presentSingle-author and collaborative research

This portfolio brings together 15 research projects developed through international research collaborations and externally funded initiatives. The funding and research collaborations originate from Malaysia, Hungary, the United Arab Emirates, and Saudi Arabia.

I served as the Statistical Lead for all 15 projects. I led the statistical and quantitative components of each project. My work covered research design, statistical methodology, econometric modelling, and computational implementation. I was also responsible for empirical analysis, robustness testing, and the interpretation of results. These responsibilities supported the development of rigorous and reproducible research outputs.

The projects address a broad range of topics. These include environmental and energy economics, macroeconomics, monetary policy, macro-finance, and applied econometrics. They apply advanced statistical and econometric methods to investigate important economic questions.
