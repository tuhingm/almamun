Which Markov-switching model fits your data?

Markov-switching models let the data date hidden regimes, such as expansions and recessions or calm and turbulent periods, and tell you how long each lasts. Find your situation in the table, then follow the guide: each one covers the number of regimes, the linearity test, regime dates, diagnostics, the checks for a collapsed regime and the text for the paper.

Which method fits?

Your situationMethod and guide
One series (GDP growth, an interest rate) whose mean shifts between statesMarkov-switching autoregression (Hamilton)
A regression whose intercept, slopes or error variance shift between statesMarkov-switching regression
Several variables whose means, volatilities or dynamics shift togetherMarkov-switching VAR
A panel of countries that share the same regimes (a global slowdown)Markov-switching panel VAR
Hidden regimes, and an observed variable (a policy rate, a spread) that changes the probability of switchingMarkov switching with time-varying transition probabilities
The regime is observed (a variable above or below a threshold)? Use the Bayesian threshold VAR.
