01 · Services

What I can help with

Each engagement is scoped around your question, your data and the decision the analysis has to support.

⌁

Policy impact & scenario analysis

Quantifying the effects of monetary, fiscal, energy and environmental policy using structural models (DSGE, heterogeneous-agent), VAR/SVAR and regime-switching methods, with clear scenarios and sensitivity checks.

↗

Econometric modelling

Time-series and panel methods (ARDL, VAR, VECM, dynamic panels), causal inference and difference-in-differences designs, and applied macro-econometric modelling.

◈

Bayesian & computational methods

Bayesian estimation, MCMC, prior specification, posterior diagnostics, and reproducible computational workflows in Python, R, Stata and Dynare.

≋

Data-driven reports & monitors

Automated, regularly updated economic reports and dashboards built on open data, designed for organisations that need timely, transparent indicators.

✓

Independent empirical review

Review of model specification, estimation, diagnostics, robustness checks, code (Stata/R/Python) and the interpretation of results before submission or publication.

Σ

Training & workshops

Applied courses for researchers and staff in econometrics, statistics, Bayesian methods and policy modelling, tailored to your team's level and software.

05 · Principles

Integrity and scope

Confidentiality

Your data and materials are used only for the agreed engagement and are not shared without your written authorisation.

Transparency

Assumptions are stated, recommendations are explained and analytical limitations are reported honestly.

Reproducibility

Where included in the agreed scope, code, procedures and analytical decisions are documented so the results can be replicated.

Advisory role

Consultation is advisory and educational: I explain methods, review specifications and code, and validate results. Clients remain the authors of their work and are responsible for their data, analysis and conclusions.

Analysis and advice are provided for information and decision support. Model-based projections depend on the data and assumptions used and are not guarantees of future outcomes. This service does not provide legal, tax or personalised investment advice.
