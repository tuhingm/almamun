04 · Research integrity

Consultation principles

Confidentiality

Research materials and datasets are handled confidentially and are not shared without authorization.

Methodological transparency

Recommendations are explained, assumptions are stated and analytical limitations are reported.

Reproducibility

Where included in the agreed scope, code, procedures and analytical decisions are documented for replication.

Academic responsibility

Consultation supports the researcher's own work. It does not include fabricated data, fabricated results or guaranteed publication outcomes. Authorship is agreed separately for genuine research collaboration.
