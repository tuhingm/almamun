Econometric readiness diagnostics

Exploratory, data-driven screening. These diagnostics do not replace formal estimator-specific tests.

Methodology and screening thresholds
  • IQR outliers: values below Q1 − 1.5 × IQR or above Q3 + 1.5 × IQR.
  • Robust MAD flags: modified z-score |0.67449 × (x − median) / MAD| greater than 3.5; not computed when MAD is zero.
  • Cross-entity correlation: mean absolute pairwise correlation above 0.50 is flagged as strong association; this is not Pesaran CD.
  • VIF: 5 or higher is elevated; 10 or higher is severe. Computed on complete numeric columns only.
  • Mean-shift scan: largest standardized difference between two segments, minimum segment size 15%; scores above 2.5 are candidates, not formal break tests.
  • Missingness: overall rate above 10% prompts review; imputation above 30% within a variable is separately warned.
  • Panel balance: each entity must contain the union of observed periods, with no missing entity/time keys or duplicate entity-time keys.

All are descriptive or heuristic screens. They do not provide estimator-specific hypothesis tests or inferential p-values.
