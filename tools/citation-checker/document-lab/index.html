3. Matching diagnostics

Testing scope

Current test build: DOCX text extraction with Mammoth.js; heuristic author–year citation detection; reference-section parsing; surname/year matching; DOI resolution against Crossref. Tables, footnotes, text boxes, unusual citation styles and complex multi-part references may not be extracted or matched perfectly.

No automatic citation replacement, reference insertion, reformatting or corrected DOCX export is enabled in this test build. Those operations require a document-preserving editing engine and explicit review controls.
