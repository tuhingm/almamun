How the tool decides. A series is treated as stationary, I(0), if the ADF test rejects a unit root in levels at 5%; as I(1) if it rejects only after first differencing. The KPSS test is reported as a cross-check, and the tool warns you when the two disagree. When every series is I(1), the Johansen trace test decides between VAR and VECM. These are standard rules, but tests have low power in short samples and can be distorted by structural breaks, so treat the result as a starting point and justify the choice in your paper.
