Which finance method fits your question?

Finance questions differ in what they ask of the data: whether risk factors explain returns, how large losses can be, how correlations move, how to allocate, which forecast is better, whether a price is a bubble. Answer one question and follow the tree to the guide that carries the method, with real returns data and the text for the paper.

Author's note

The decision path, the choice of tests and diagnostics, and the inference design in this guide are my own. I built them from my training and research in econometrics, and I implemented and checked every step myself in Python. The estimators are standard methods from the literature, cited where they are used.

— Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun, Ph.D.
