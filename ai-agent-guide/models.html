01The dictionary

The dictionary is the framework's memory of canonical models. Each entry records a paper's variables, lags, identification and sample, and pairs with a replication script that has to reproduce numbers from the paper. Mode A runs an entry, Mode B drafts a new one, and Mode C searches them.

It currently holds 37 entries: 30 written and checked against the papers by the BIS authors, and 7 drafts that I added.

02Added models

These seven are in my copy only. Their library code is tested by recovering known parameters from simulated data, for example a nonlinear ARDL that recovers asymmetric long-run effects of 1.5 and 0.6 as 1.49 and 0.59. They are drafts: I wrote the entries from knowledge of the methods and have not yet checked them against the papers, so they carry no page references.

Limits

  • The Markov-switching VAR has no analytic standard errors (a bootstrap is provided) and no regime-dependent impulse responses.
  • The threshold VAR has two regimes and gives regime-conditional, not generalised, impulse responses.
  • The smooth-transition model is univariate, and its steepness parameter is weakly identified when the transition is nearly a step.
  • The nonlinear ARDL handles one asymmetric regressor.
