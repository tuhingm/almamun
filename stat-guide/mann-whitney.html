Mann-Whitney U test

The Mann-Whitney U test compares two independent samples through their ranks and tests whether one tends to give larger values than the other.

Overview

The Mann-Whitney U test compares two independent samples through their ranks and tests whether one tends to give larger values than the other.

Validated Compared with reference software on identical data and specifications, and checked by Monte Carlo simulation.

Run it on your dataTechnical validation report

Mathematical formulation

U_1 = R_1 − n_1(n_1 + 1)/2, where R_1 is the rank sum of the first sample in the pooled data; U_2 = n_1 n_2 − U_1.
Exact null distribution (no ties): P(U = u) = c(u; n_1, n_2) / C(n_1 + n_2, n_1), c from the Gaussian binomial coefficient.
Normal approximation: z = (U − n_1n_2/2 ∓ 0.5) / σ_U with σ_U² = (n_1n_2/12) [(N + 1) − Σ(t³ − t)/(N(N − 1))] (tie-corrected).
Rank-biserial correlation r = 1 − 2U_1/(n_1n_2) (sign follows the first group); Hodges-Lehmann shift = median of all n_1n_2 pairwise differences, with a confidence interval from order statistics of those differences.

Assumptions

  • Independent observations in two groups.
  • An ordinal or continuous outcome.
  • For the shift interpretation: the two distributions have the same shape. Without that, the test addresses P(X > Y) ≠ 1/2, not equality of medians.

Estimation algorithm

  • Mid-ranks of the pooled sample; exact enumeration by counting when no ties and n small; otherwise the tie-corrected normal approximation with optional continuity correction.

Diagnostics and interpretation

  • The rank-biserial correlation is the effect size: 0 means no tendency, ±1 complete separation.
  • A significant result means the probability that a draw from one group exceeds a draw from the other is not 1/2.

The diagnostic tables are part of the results above (assumption checks, fit statistics, residual or goodness-of-fit tables).

Limitations

  • With unequal spreads the test is no longer a test of location and its Type I error for 'equal medians' can exceed the nominal level.
  • The exact test is skipped when ties are present.
