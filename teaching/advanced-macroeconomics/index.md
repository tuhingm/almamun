# Advanced Macroeconomics
## Graduate course in dynamic macroeconomic theory and policy

**Prerequisites:** Intermediate macroeconomics, calculus, linear algebra, probability, and introductory econometrics.

## Course description
A graduate treatment of dynamic general-equilibrium macroeconomics, growth, business cycles, nominal rigidities, monetary and fiscal policy, open-economy dynamics, heterogeneous-agent models, and quantitative DSGE methods.

## Learning outcomes
Students will derive intertemporal household and firm optimality conditions; characterize competitive and recursive equilibrium; analyze steady states, transitional dynamics, and rational-expectations determinacy; construct and solve RBC, New Keynesian, and heterogeneous-agent models; calibrate and estimate DSGE models; and conduct transparent policy counterfactuals and welfare analysis.
