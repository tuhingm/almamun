Advanced Macroeconomics · Course 04

Readings

Dynamic macroeconomic theory and quantitative policy analysis — from the Euler equation to HANK.

COURSE INFO

14 lecture sessions · Graduate

InstructorTuhin G. M. Al Mamun, Ph.D.
PrerequisitesIntermediate macroeconomics, calculus, linear algebra, probability, introductory econometrics
SoftwareMATLAB, Dynare, Julia, Python
Author's note

The design of this course, the lecture notes, the worked examples and the problem sets are my own. I prepared them from my training, research and teaching in economics. The theory and methods are standard, and the sources are listed in each lecture's readings.

— Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun, Ph.D.

Readings are listed by session. Core textbooks for the whole course are listed at the end.

Lecture 1: The Dynamic Household: Intertemporal Choice and Euler Equations

Ljungqvist and Sargent, Recursive Macroeconomic Theory; Deaton, Understanding Consumption.

Lecture 2: Firms, Capital Accumulation, and Investment

Hayashi (1982), “Tobin's Marginal q and Average q”; Ljungqvist and Sargent, Recursive Macroeconomic Theory.

Lecture 3: Solow and Ramsey Growth: Steady States and Transitional Dynamics

Acemoglu, Introduction to Modern Economic Growth; Barro and Sala-i-Martin, Economic Growth.

Lecture 4: Real Business Cycle Models and Competitive Equilibrium

King, Plosser and Rebelo (1988); Heer and Maussner, Dynamic General Equilibrium Modeling.

Lecture 5: Nominal Rigidities, Calvo Pricing, and the New Keynesian Phillips Curve

Galí, Monetary Policy, Inflation, and the Business Cycle; Woodford, Interest and Prices.

Lecture 6: The Three-Equation New Keynesian Model

Galí, Monetary Policy, Inflation, and the Business Cycle; Clarida, Galí and Gertler (1999).

Lecture 7: Monetary Policy: Rules, Commitment, and the Effective Lower Bound

Woodford, Interest and Prices; Clarida, Galí and Gertler (1999); Galí, Monetary Policy, Inflation, and the Business Cycle.

Lecture 8: Fiscal Policy, Public Debt, and Monetary-Fiscal Interactions

Leeper (1991); Woodford, Interest and Prices; Blanchard, Macroeconomics.

Lecture 9: Open-Economy Macroeconomics and Exchange-Rate Dynamics

Obstfeld and Rogoff, Foundations of International Macroeconomics; Galí and Monacelli (2005).

Lecture 10: DSGE Solution Methods: Linearization, Perturbation, and QZ

Klein (2000); Sims (2002); Heer and Maussner, Dynamic General Equilibrium Modeling.

Lecture 11: DSGE Estimation: Likelihood, Bayesian Inference, and Model Evaluation

An and Schorfheide (2007); Fernández-Villaverde (2010); Herbst and Schorfheide, Bayesian Estimation of DSGE Models.

Lecture 12: Heterogeneous-Agent Macroeconomics and Incomplete Markets

Aiyagari (1994); Huggett (1993); Ljungqvist and Sargent, Recursive Macroeconomic Theory.

Lecture 13: HANK Models: Distribution Dynamics and Aggregate Transmission

Kaplan, Moll and Violante (2018); Auclert et al. (2021); Achdou et al. (2022).

Lecture 14: Quantitative Macro Policy Analysis, Validation, and Replication

Smets and Wouters (2007); Fernández-Villaverde (2010); Del Negro and Schorfheide, DSGE Models in Macroeconomics.
