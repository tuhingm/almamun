# Calculus — Examination Specifications

## Midterm examination
**Coverage:** Lectures 1–7  
**Duration:** 120 minutes  
**Weight:** 20%

### Structure
1. Epsilon-delta limit or continuity proof.
2. Sequence or series convergence argument.
3. Differentiation and mean value theorem application.
4. Taylor approximation and single-variable optimization.
5. Definite or improper integration.
6. Multivariable limit, partial derivative, or chain-rule calculation.

### Evaluation criteria
Correct definitions and assumptions; valid derivations; logical structure; accurate calculations; interpretation where relevant.

## Final examination
**Coverage:** Lectures 8–14, with cumulative concepts from earlier lectures  
**Duration:** 180 minutes  
**Weight:** 25%

### Structure
1. Hessian classification and unconstrained optimization.
2. Lagrange multiplier problem with economic interpretation.
3. KKT problem with active constraints and complementary slackness.
4. Implicit-function comparative statics.
5. Multiple integral and Jacobian transformation.
6. Differential-equation steady state and local stability.
7. Difference-equation fixed point and dynamic stability.

### Evaluation criteria
Correct formulation; explicit regularity and constraint assumptions; mathematically valid solution; interpretation of multipliers and stability; clear notation.

## Examination policy
Students must show intermediate steps. Unsupported final answers receive limited credit. Numerical computation may be used only where explicitly permitted and must not replace required analytical reasoning.
