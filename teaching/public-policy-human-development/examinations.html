ASSESSMENT SPECIFICATION

Examinations

Assessment tests conceptual understanding, policy reasoning, evidence interpretation and communication.

Midterm

  • Short conceptual explanations.
  • Interpretation of a human-development indicator or policy case.
  • Structured comparison of policy instruments.

Final assessment

  • Integrative questions across the course modules.
  • Analysis of a country or subnational policy case.
  • Critical discussion of assumptions, implementation and limitations.
