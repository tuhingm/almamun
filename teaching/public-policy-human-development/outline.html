Topic Outline

Module 1: Foundations of Public Policy

Policy problems, agenda setting, formulation, adoption, implementation and evaluation; policy actors and instruments.

Applied focus: Map a social problem through the policy cycle and identify actors, instruments and implementation constraints.

Module 2: Human Development: Concepts and Frameworks

Human development, capabilities, functionings, agency, well-being and the distinction between income and human progress.

Applied focus: Use the capability approach to distinguish resources, achieved functionings and substantive opportunities.

Module 3: Measuring Human Development

HDI dimensions, life expectancy, education indicators, income measures, inequality-adjusted indicators and data comparability.

Applied focus: Interpret a composite index and explain how normalization and aggregation affect comparisons.

Module 4: Poverty, Vulnerability and Multidimensional Deprivation

Income and consumption poverty, poverty lines, headcount and poverty gap, multidimensional poverty and vulnerability.

Applied focus: Calculate headcount and poverty-gap measures and identify deprivations that income measures may miss.

Module 5: Inequality, Social Exclusion and Inclusive Development

Lorenz curves, Gini coefficient, distributional shares, horizontal inequality, social exclusion and intergenerational mobility.

Applied focus: Compare inequality measures and explain why average income growth may not imply inclusive development.

Module 6: Education Policy and Human Capital

Access, quality, learning outcomes, financing, teacher policy, early childhood development and returns to education.

Applied focus: Construct a policy logic model linking education inputs to access, learning and longer-run outcomes.

Module 7: Health Policy and Population Well-being

Universal health coverage, health financing, primary care, prevention, health equity and social determinants of health.

Applied focus: Compare financing and delivery instruments using access, quality, equity and financial-protection criteria.

Module 8: Employment, Labor Markets and Decent Work

Labor-force participation, informality, unemployment, skills, wage quality, labor standards and active labor-market policies.

Applied focus: Diagnose a labor-market problem and match it to demand-side, supply-side or institutional interventions.

Module 9: Social Protection and Welfare Systems

Social assistance, social insurance, pensions, child benefits, targeting, universalism and adaptive social protection.

Applied focus: Compare targeted and universal transfer designs using coverage, adequacy, exclusion and administrative costs.

Module 10: Gender, Demography and Human Development

Gender equality, unpaid care, demographic transition, migration, youth, aging and population policy.

Applied focus: Use a gender and life-cycle lens to identify unequal exposure to policy costs and benefits.

Module 11: Governance, Institutions and Policy Implementation

State capacity, accountability, decentralization, public financial management, service delivery and policy coordination.

Applied focus: Trace how implementation capacity and institutional incentives affect frontline service delivery.

Module 12: Sustainable Development and Integrated Policy Design

SDGs, climate resilience, energy access, just transition, intergenerational equity and policy coherence.

Applied focus: Develop an integrated policy framework connecting environmental transition with distributional and social outcomes.

Module 13: Climate Change, Energy Transition and Social Development

Energy access, affordability, employment transitions, energy poverty, distributional effects, climate vulnerability and just-transition policy.

Applied focus: Build a social-impact framework linking energy pathways to household welfare, labor markets and regional inequality.

Module 14: Policy Evaluation, Evidence and Impact Assessment

Theory of change, logic models, monitoring indicators, randomized evaluations, quasi-experimental designs, process evaluation and evidence synthesis.

Applied focus: Select an evaluation design for a policy question and distinguish implementation monitoring from impact evaluation.

Module 15: Integrated Policy Lab and Human Development Strategy

Problem diagnosis, stakeholder mapping, policy alternatives, fiscal and administrative feasibility, distributional analysis, SDG alignment and policy-brief communication.

Applied focus: Develop an integrated policy proposal with measurable outcomes, implementation responsibilities and an evaluation plan.
