About this course

An interdisciplinary graduate course examining how public institutions and policy choices shape human capabilities and well-being. It covers human-development frameworks and measurement, poverty and inequality, education and health systems, employment, social protection, gender, governance and sustainable development, using comparative cases and applied policy exercises.

What you will learn

  1. Explain major approaches to human development, capabilities and well-being.
  2. Interpret human-development indicators and assess their measurement limits.
  3. Analyze poverty, inequality and social exclusion using multidimensional perspectives.
  4. Compare policy instruments in education, health, employment and social protection.
  5. Assess how institutions, governance and implementation shape policy outcomes.
  6. Develop an evidence-informed policy brief connecting a defined social problem to feasible policy options.

Course organization

Each session includes learning objectives, conceptual development, an applied example, discussion questions and independent exercises. Case studies and public data exercises connect the course to contemporary policy debates.

Module 01 Lecture 1

  1. Foundations of Public Policy

Policy problems, agenda setting, formulation, adoption, implementation and evaluation; policy actors and instruments.

Module 02 Lecture 2

  1. Human Development: Concepts and Frameworks

Human development, capabilities, functionings, agency, well-being and the distinction between income and human progress.

Module 03 Lecture 3

  1. Measuring Human Development

HDI dimensions, life expectancy, education indicators, income measures, inequality-adjusted indicators and data comparability.

Module 04 Lecture 4

  1. Poverty, Vulnerability and Multidimensional Deprivation

Income and consumption poverty, poverty lines, headcount and poverty gap, multidimensional poverty and vulnerability.

Module 05 Lecture 5

  1. Inequality, Social Exclusion and Inclusive Development

Lorenz curves, Gini coefficient, distributional shares, horizontal inequality, social exclusion and intergenerational mobility.

Module 06 Lecture 6

  1. Education Policy and Human Capital

Access, quality, learning outcomes, financing, teacher policy, early childhood development and returns to education.

Module 07 Lecture 7

  1. Health Policy and Population Well-being

Universal health coverage, health financing, primary care, prevention, health equity and social determinants of health.

Module 08 Lecture 8

  1. Employment, Labor Markets and Decent Work

Labor-force participation, informality, unemployment, skills, wage quality, labor standards and active labor-market policies.

Module 09 Lecture 9

  1. Social Protection and Welfare Systems

Social assistance, social insurance, pensions, child benefits, targeting, universalism and adaptive social protection.

Module 10 Lecture 10

  1. Gender, Demography and Human Development

Gender equality, unpaid care, demographic transition, migration, youth, aging and population policy.

Module 11 Lecture 11

  1. Governance, Institutions and Policy Implementation

State capacity, accountability, decentralization, public financial management, service delivery and policy coordination.

Module 12 Lecture 12

  1. Sustainable Development and Integrated Policy Design

SDGs, climate resilience, energy access, just transition, intergenerational equity and policy coherence.

Assessment framework

Suggested assessment: participation and preparation 10%; problem sets and case exercises 25%; applied policy assignments 25%; midterm assessment 15%; final policy brief or examination 25%. Instructors may adapt the weighting.
