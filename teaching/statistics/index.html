01 / OVERVIEW

About this course

A mathematical and applied course in probability, statistical inference, Bayesian methods, experimental design, regression, generalized linear models, nonparametric methods, power, mixed models, and multivariate analysis.

What you will learn

Students will formulate probability models; derive estimators and sampling distributions; conduct likelihood and Bayesian inference; design experiments; analyze factorial and hierarchical data; fit GLMs; plan power; and produce reproducible statistical analyses.

How the course is organized

Each lecture session has a full set of written notes: learning objectives, formal development with derivations, worked examples, a problem set, a computational lab and readings. Problem sets with solution guidance and examination specifications are under Assignments and Exams.

Part I · Probability and asymptotics Lectures 1–3

  1. Probability Models, Random Variables, and Expectation
  2. Probability Distributions and Multivariate Models
  3. Sampling Distributions and Asymptotic Theory

Part II · Estimation and inference Lectures 4–6

  1. Point Estimation: Moments, Likelihood, and Optimality
  2. Confidence Intervals and Hypothesis Testing
  3. Bayesian Inference, Conjugacy, and Posterior Computation

Part III · Experimental design and ANOVA Lectures 7–8

  1. Experimental Design, Randomization, and Blocking
  2. ANOVA, Linear Contrasts, and Multiple Comparisons

Part IV · Statistical modeling Lectures 9–13

  1. Linear Regression and Generalized Linear Models
  2. Nonparametric Inference, Rank Tests, and Resampling
  3. Statistical Power, Sample Size, and Equivalence
  4. Linear Mixed Models and Hierarchical Data
  5. Multivariate Analysis: PCA, Covariance, and Dimension Reduction

Part V · Statistical workflow Lecture 14

  1. Statistical Modeling Workflow, Simulation, and Reproducibility

Assessment

Problem sets 25%; design/power report 20%; computational labs 20%; midterm 15%; final project 20%.

Core references

Casella and Berger, Statistical Inference; Lehmann and Romano, Testing Statistical Hypotheses; van der Vaart, Asymptotic Statistics; Gelman et al., Bayesian Data Analysis; Montgomery, Design and Analysis of Experiments; McCullagh and Nelder, Generalized Linear Models.
