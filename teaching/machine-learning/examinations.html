ASSESSMENT SPECIFICATION

Examinations

Assessment should test conceptual understanding, application, interpretation and communication.

Midterm assessment

  • Short conceptual explanations.
  • Structured analytical or computational problems.
  • Interpretation of a table, model output or policy case.

Final assessment

  • Integrative questions spanning multiple course modules.
  • Applied case analysis or model assessment.
  • Critical discussion of assumptions, uncertainty and limitations.
