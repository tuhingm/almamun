# Finance
## Graduate and advanced undergraduate course in financial theory and markets

**Prerequisites:** Calculus, linear algebra, probability, statistics, and introductory economics.

## Course description
A rigorous introduction to asset pricing, portfolio choice, fixed-income valuation, derivatives, corporate finance, financial institutions, risk management, behavioral finance, and empirical financial econometrics.

## Learning outcomes
Students will be able to:
- Measure financial returns and characterize risk.
- Derive portfolio allocations and asset-pricing restrictions.
- Price bonds and derivatives using no-arbitrage methods.
- Evaluate investment projects and corporate financing choices.
- Analyze banking, systemic risk, and portfolio risk.
- Estimate financial models and design reproducible empirical studies.
