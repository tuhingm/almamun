Finance · Course 05

Lecture Notes

Financial theory, asset pricing, corporate finance and financial econometrics.

COURSE INFO

15 lecture sessions · Advanced undergraduate / Graduate

InstructorTuhin G. M. Al Mamun, Ph.D.
PrerequisitesCalculus, linear algebra, probability, statistics, introductory economics
SoftwarePython, R
Author's note

The design of this course, the lecture notes, the worked examples and the problem sets are my own. I prepared them from my training, research and teaching in economics. The theory and methods are standard, and the sources are listed in each lecture's readings.

— Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun, Ph.D.

Complete written notes for each session. Every set of notes opens with learning objectives and closes with a problem set, a computational lab and readings. Use Print / save as PDF on any lecture page for an offline copy.

Lec #Topic and objectivesLength
Part I · Risk, return and asset pricing
1Returns, Risk, and the Foundations of Asset Pricing
Measure simple and log returns; distinguish arithmetic and geometric performance; quantify risk and explain the role of stochastic discounting.
8 min read
2Risk, Probability, and Expected Utility
Represent uncertainty with probability distributions; derive expected utility and certainty equivalents; understand risk aversion and downside risk.
8 min read
3Mean-Variance Portfolio Theory and the Efficient Frontier
Derive minimum-variance portfolios and efficient frontiers; understand diversification, tangency portfolios, and estimation sensitivity.
8 min read
4CAPM, Beta, and Empirical Factor Models
Derive CAPM implications; estimate beta and alpha; interpret factor models and distinguish systematic from idiosyncratic risk.
8 min read
5Arbitrage Pricing, Stochastic Discount Factors, and No-Arbitrage
Use no-arbitrage restrictions to price payoffs; derive SDF relations; understand factor pricing and risk-premium covariance.
8 min read
Part II · Fixed income and derivatives
6Fixed-Income Securities, Bond Pricing, and Yield Curves
Price fixed cash flows; distinguish coupon, spot, forward, and yield-to-maturity rates; understand credit spreads and discount curves.
7 min read
7Duration, Convexity, Immunization, and Term Structure
Measure bond price sensitivity; derive duration and convexity approximations; understand immunization and term-structure models.
8 min read
8Forwards, Futures, Options, and Swaps
Value derivative payoffs; derive put-call parity and binomial pricing; distinguish physical and risk-neutral probabilities; interpret Greeks and hedging.
10 min read
Part III · Corporate finance and markets
9Corporate Finance: Capital Budgeting and Project Valuation
Value investment projects using incremental cash flows; calculate NPV and IRR; apply WACC consistently; evaluate uncertainty and real options.
8 min read
10Capital Structure, Leverage, and Payout Policy
Understand Modigliani-Miller propositions; derive tax-shield effects; compare trade-off and pecking-order theories; analyze dividends and repurchases.
8 min read
11Market Efficiency, Information, Governance, and Event Studies
Distinguish forms of market efficiency; design event studies; understand information asymmetry, governance, and limits to arbitrage.
7 min read
Part IV · Banking and risk
12Banking, Financial Intermediation, and Systemic Risk
Analyze bank balance sheets, maturity transformation, capital and liquidity risk, and mechanisms of systemic instability.
8 min read
13Financial Risk Management: VaR, Expected Shortfall, and Stress Testing
Quantify market and credit risk; estimate VaR and expected shortfall; understand tail dependence, model risk, and stress testing.
8 min read
Part V · Behavioral and empirical finance
14Behavioral Finance: Beliefs, Preferences, and Market Outcomes
Understand behavioral departures from benchmark rational choice; connect psychological mechanisms to asset prices and trading behavior.
8 min read
15Empirical Finance and Financial Econometrics
Estimate return and factor models; model conditional volatility; design event studies; evaluate predictability with careful data controls.
9 min read
