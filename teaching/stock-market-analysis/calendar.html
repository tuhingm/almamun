Calendar

A 15-week training course in price action analysis of stocks, from reading the daily chart to a written trading plan.

COURSE INFO

15 weekly sessions · Practitioner level

InstructorTuhin G. M. Al Mamun, Ph.D.
PrerequisitesNone. A broker demo account is useful from Week 1.
SoftwareAny charting platform that shows daily candlestick charts
Author's note

The design of this training course, the lessons, the worked examples and the exercises are my own. All charts use simulated prices that I made for teaching, and they are not real stocks. This course is general education and is not investment advice.

— Tuhin G. M. Al Mamun, Ph.D.

One session per week for 15 weeks. Each week ends with a chart lab to do on your own charts before the next session.

Ses #TopicChart lab
Part I · Reading the market
1How Stocks Are Traded: Brokers and OrdersOpen a demo account and place one order of each type
2Reading Price Bars and Time FramesLabel every part of ten bars on a daily chart
3Trend and Swing PointsMark the swing points and classify the trend on five stocks
Part II · Key levels and the 21 EMA
4Key Levels: Support and ResistanceDraw the key levels on three daily charts
5The 21 EMA as Dynamic Support and ResistanceFind three retracements that ended at the 21 EMA
Part III · The three signals
6The Pin BarFind and mark ten pin bars; keep only those at a key level
7The Inside BarFind five inside bars and write the entry, stop loss and target
8The FakeyFind three fakeys and compare them with the inside bars that did not fail
Part IV · Putting the trade together
9Trading with the Trend: The Retracement EntryPlan two trades with the trend, on paper
10Sideways Markets, Reversals and Counter-Trend TradesClassify five charts as trend or sideways and list the risks of counter-trend trades
11ConfluenceCount the confluence on ten setups and keep only the best
Part V · Risk and trade management
12Stop Loss, Risk Reward and Position SizingFill in the position size table for your own account
13Trade ManagementReplay three trades and manage each one with the rules
Part VI · Plan, routine and going live
14Gaps, News and the Daily RoutineWrite your daily routine and run it for five days
15The Trading Plan, Psychology and Going LiveComplete the trading plan and the first 20-trade journal
