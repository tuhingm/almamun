Description and aims

A 15-week training course in price action analysis of stocks. It is written for people who can already open a chart and want a clear, rule-based way to read it. The course is a training programme, not a degree course, and the aim is a written trading plan that you can follow with discipline.

Who it is for

  • Beginners who want a method that uses the chart and little else.
  • Self-taught traders who want rules for entries, stop loss, target and position size.
  • Students of finance who want to see how price behaves on a daily chart.

What you need

  • A charting platform that shows daily and weekly candlestick charts of stocks.
  • A broker demo account. Use it for the first weeks of practice.
  • A notebook or spreadsheet for the trading journal.

Weekly outline

Week Topic Chart lab
1 How stocks are traded: brokers and orders Open a demo account and place one order of each type
2 Reading price bars and time frames Label every part of ten bars on a daily chart
3 Trend and swing points Mark the swing points and classify the trend on five stocks
4 Key levels: support and resistance Draw the key levels on three daily charts
5 The 21 EMA as dynamic support and resistance Find three retracements that ended at the 21 EMA
6 The pin bar Find and mark ten pin bars; keep only those at a key level
7 The inside bar Find five inside bars and write the entry, stop loss and target
8 The fakey Find three fakeys and compare them with the inside bars that did not fail
9 Trading with the trend: the retracement entry Plan two trades with the trend, on paper
10 Sideways markets, reversals and counter-trend trades Classify five charts as trend or sideways and list the risks of counter-trend trades
11 Confluence Count the confluence on ten setups and keep only the best
12 Stop loss, risk reward and position sizing Fill in the position size table for your own account
13 Trade management Replay three trades and manage each one with the rules
14 Gaps, news and the daily routine Write your daily routine and run it for five days
15 The trading plan, psychology and going live Complete the trading plan and the first 20-trade journal

Assessment

There are no grades. The course is complete when you have a finished trading plan, a trading journal with at least 20 trades on a demo account, and a written review of those trades.

Risk notice

This course is general education and not investment advice. Trading stocks carries a risk of loss. All charts in the course use simulated prices made for teaching. Results shown, real or simulated, are no promise of future results.
