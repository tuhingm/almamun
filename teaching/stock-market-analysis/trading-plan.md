# Trading Plan and Journal

Complete this plan during the course. Write it in your own words, and keep it short enough that you will read it before every trade. Change it only after a review of your trading journal, never in the middle of a trade.

## Part 1: My trading plan

**1. Why I trade, and what I expect.** Write the goal in plain words. Include the amount of time you can give each day.

**2. The account.** Broker, account size, and the money you can afford to lose.

**3. The time frames.** The weekly chart for the trend, the daily chart for the key levels and signals.

**4. What I trade.** The kind of stocks I will choose, and how many I will follow at one time.

**5. The setups.** The signals I trade, and where each must appear.

- Pin bar at a key level, with the trend or at a clear reversal
- Inside bar, broken in the direction of the trend
- Fakey, with the trend
- Minimum confluence needed before I enter (write the number)

**6. Entry.** Where the order goes, and which order type I use.

**7. Stop loss.** Where it goes for each signal, and the rule that I never move it further away.

**8. Target and risk reward.** The smallest risk reward I accept (write the ratio).

**9. Position size.** Risk per trade as a percentage of the account, and the formula.

**10. Trade management.** When I move the stop loss to breakeven, and how I trail it.

**11. News and gaps.** What I do when news is due, and when I stay out.

**12. Rules for stopping.** The number of losing trades in a row after which I stop and review.

**13. Psychology.** The emotion I notice most, and what I do about it.

**14. Daily routine.** The six steps from the daily routine chart.
